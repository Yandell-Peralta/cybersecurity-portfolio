## Overview

Completed the TryHackMe "Systems as Attack Vectors" room.

## What I Did

## What I Learned

- Vulnerabilities can provide attackers with opportunities to compromise systems
- Misconfigured systems can create security risks
- Attackers can target different components of an organization's systems
- SOC analysts help monitor, investigate, and respond to system-based threats
