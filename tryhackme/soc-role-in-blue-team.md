# TryHackMe — SOC Role in Blue Team

## Overview

Completed the TryHackMe "SOC Role in Blue Team" room.

This room introduced the role of the Security Operations Center (SOC) within an organization's security structure and explored different defensive security roles.

## What I Did

- Learned about the purpose of a Blue Team.
- Learned how a SOC fits into an organization's security structure.
- Reviewed the responsibilities of SOC Level 1 and Level 2 analysts.
- Learned about incident response teams and specialized security roles.
- Learned about the role of a CISO and security management.
- Compared internal SOC teams with Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs).
- Completed a challenge involving different security roles and security incidents.

## What I Learned

- Blue Teams focus on defending organizations from cyber threats.
- SOC analysts monitor, investigate, and respond to security alerts.
- SOC Level 1 analysts typically triage alerts and escalate more complex incidents.
- Larger organizations may have specialized teams such as incident response, threat intelligence, and digital forensics.
- Security teams work together to detect, investigate, and respond to incidents.
- A SOC can be either part of an organization or provided through an MSSP.
