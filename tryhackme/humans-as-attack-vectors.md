# TryHackMe — Humans as Attack Vectors

## Overview

Completed the TryHackMe "Humans as Attack Vectors" room.

This room focused on how attackers target humans and how security teams detect and defend against these attacks.

## What I Did

- Learned about the human element in cybersecurity
- Studied common attacks targeting people
- Practiced identifying and defending against human-based attacks
- Explored the role of a SOC in detecting and mitigating these attacks

## What I Learned

- Humans can be a major attack vector in cybersecurity
- Attackers use social engineering to manipulate users
- Security awareness and defensive controls can reduce risk
- SOC analysts play an important role in detecting and responding to these attacks
